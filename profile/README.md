## 🤝 Values

We build and maintain the bots, tools, and website that let Eufurria’s members find each other, organise events, and feel at home in the community, on Discord, on Telegram, and on the web.

Concretely, that means:

- Keeping the website ([eufurria.org](https://eufurria.org)) accurate, welcoming, and easy to navigate in both PT and EN.
- Building and maintaining bots that make Discord and Telegram feel less like generic servers/groups and more like *Eufurria*.
- Making sure new IT/dev volunteers can find their footing quickly, so the work doesn’t bottleneck on any one person.
- Watching over the platforms’ health (uptime, security, backups) so members can trust them without thinking about it.

## ⭐ North star

**Users, always keep our users in the frontline of our development efforts.**

We must think as members, not as staff or volunteers.

What would help? What can we improve? How can we remove friction? That’s our goal.

***Every decision, what we build, what we document, what we prioritise, should trace back to this.***

If a change doesn’t make things better for the people using the website, Discord, or Telegram, it’s not worth doing yet
